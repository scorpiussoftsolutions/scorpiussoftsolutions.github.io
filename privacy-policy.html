Arrow Puzzler Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzler is provided by Scorpius Soft Solutions. This policy applies to the Android game.

Game progress

The game stores your level progress, coins, unlocks and preferences on your device. The game does not require an account. You can remove this local information by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

Advertising and consent

We use Google AdMob for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and advertising identifiers, ad or app interactions, and diagnostics. Google uses this information for functions such as serving and measuring ads, analytics, and preventing fraud. Google says its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it collects in transit.

Where required, Google’s consent dialog appears before ad requests. When ad privacy options are available, you can reopen them from the in-game Store. Android also provides advertising identifier controls in system settings.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy for details about how Google handles and retains data. Contact us for requests about information we hold. Requests about information Google holds may need to be handled by Google.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use and retain that information as needed to respond and resolve the request, and where required by law.
